# Writing Cross-Domain Design

`design/` holds the domain map, cross-domain architecture and conventions,
and decision records. Feature-local design belongs beside its product
specification in `specs/`.

- Follow the [shared writing rules](../eng/context-management.md#writing-rules)
  and [design writing rules](../eng/context-management.md#design-specification-writing).
- Keep a cross-domain fact here only when it governs more than one feature.
  Link to feature contracts instead of repeating them.
- Follow the [decision-record rules](../eng/context-management.md#decision-records)
  for ADRs. Keep the README for human navigation.
