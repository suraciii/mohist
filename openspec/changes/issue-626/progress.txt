## Codebase Patterns
- `WorkExecutor` owns the Action execution boundary: it deep-clones `DispatchWorkItem.with`, injects engine inputs, checks unresolved references, renders deferred fields, validates, resolves the workspace directory, constructs cleanup input, and invokes the handler.
- `ownerKind === 'agent-job'` is the only non-Workflow owner branch. Current Workflow dispatches use `workflow`; legacy envelopes may omit `ownerKind` and parse as `undefined`.
- `tryRecovery` deep-clones the raw `with`, artifacts, setVars, recovery, and expect declarations for `retrySelf`, derives the parent continuation id, and decrements `recoveryRemaining` once. The continuation is normalized again when it executes.
- The current `core/script` manifest declares required `run` and optional `shell` and `timeout`; the catalog projects those inputs and must not expose retired `resourceProfile`.
