### MF-4 — prompt-owner Connection is not re-authorized under its current policy at click time — FIXED

The pending-click pipeline now re-authorizes both roles before winner commit:
first the prompt-owner Connection under the route-validated delivering lease
context, then the selected Connection under its separately resolved current
lease. Both evaluations use `SlackConnectionAccessDecider`; either denial is a
visible `unauthorized` result and creates no selection mutation, winner,
provider inbox entry, Session, Turn, or AgentJob. When both roles name the same
Connection, one equivalent current lease-and-policy evaluation may satisfy
both.

- proposal.md: click-time behavior and capability summary now require separate
  current-policy authorization for prompt owner and selected Connection under
  each Connection's own current lease.
- design.md: Goal and Decision 4 add prompt-owner authorization as pipeline
  step 6, before candidate handling and commit; selected lease/authorization
  move to steps 8-9; the no-mutation boundary covers steps 1-10. Decision 8
  passes the route-validated `SlackLeaseContext` to selection handling for the
  prompt-owner decider call while explicitly retaining separate selected-
  Connection lease resolution. The route lease gate is distinguished from the
  mutable access-policy decision.
- specs/slack-agent-selection-action: new prompt-owner authorization
  requirement and deterministic scenarios cover policy narrowing, allowlist
  removal, live-member loss/unverifiable identity, channel-membership loss/
  unverifiable conversation, and same-Connection evaluation de-duplication.
  Route and rejection requirements now pin the lease-context handoff and the
  no-winner/no-resource result.
- tasks.json T-003: description, acceptance criteria, and fixed-time test
  matrix now require both current authorizations and all prompt-owner mutable-
  policy scenarios.
