## Codebase Patterns

- `useWorkflowTimeline` is consumed by the issue detail workflow surface and Activity timeline; its query key is under the `['issues']` prefix, so existing domain invalidations refresh it without a separate page-local cache path.
- `useEventsConnection` increments `reconnectVersion` only after `onreconnected`; `LiveTaskProvider` now forwards that value as `eventsReconnectVersion` through `LiveTaskState`.
- Task and artifact reverse-DNS events are already in `EVENT_TYPES` and subscribed by the events hub. Their typed payloads must be added to `entities/issue/@x/events.ts` before adding `ROUTE` rows so `_AssertRouteSubscribes` remains meaningful.
- Issue detail uses a first-load-only `isLoading || !issue` guard and identity keys in its workflow/reference sections. No `isFetching` guard or new remount key was introduced, preserving local collapse state and reading position.
