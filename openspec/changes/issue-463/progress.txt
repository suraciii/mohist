## Codebase Patterns

- Transcript event delivery subscriptions come from `TRANSCRIPT_EVENT_TYPES`; `EVENT_TYPES` is sent to SignalR and `AGENT_DETAIL_ROUTED_EVENT_TYPES` feeds the typed in-process event bus.
- Transcript terminal rendering belongs in `useSessionTranscript`; reverse-DNS `ROUTE` rows are not used for transcript events.
- Session-wide `session.closed` uses finalizing presentation, while operation-scoped follow-up terminals invalidate session queries without marking the session finalizing or globally terminal.
- Workflow session cards converge by invalidating the workflow-run sessions query; agent activity converges through `AGENT_ACTIVITY_EVENT_NAMES` in `routeEvent`.
