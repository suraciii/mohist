## Codebase patterns

- Widgets under `packages/web/src/widgets/<name>/` use `ui/` for components, `model/` for pure derivation, and `index.ts` as barrel.
- `useProject()` is required because `useIssues`/`useAgentStatus` are enabled only when `projectId` is present; component mirrors `AttentionHero` in passing `projectId` to `useIssues`.
- Tests use `vi.setSystemTime()` + relative ISO strings so local-calendar-day assertions remain stable across timezones.
- When asserting DOM order, `Node.DOCUMENT_POSITION_FOLLOWING` works in jsdom.
- Avoid wrapping `AttentionHero` in `DashboardZone` to prevent duplicate `dashboard-zone-attention` testids and preserve its distinct Hero styling.
