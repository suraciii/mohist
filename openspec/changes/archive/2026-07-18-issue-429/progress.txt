## Codebase Patterns

- `TurnList` keeps every turn and tool row mounted; per-turn `contentVisibility: 'auto'` preserves DOM anchor lookup while deferring browser layout/paint.
- A `3rem` per-turn `containIntrinsicSize` matches the turn divider's minimum footprint and provides a stable off-screen placeholder.
- jsdom verification for this strategy is structural: assert styles and `querySelector` anchor/expansion behavior; real off-screen painting remains browser-only.
