## Codebase Patterns

- `IssueQuerier` is the narrow Issue read-side boundary and may deserialize Issue state for fields such as body without expanding the full `IssueReadModel` collections.
- `RunnerRoutes` is the cross-domain composition boundary for poll HTTP DTOs; `Mohist.Server.Runner` must not depend on `Mohist.Server.Issue`, and Orleans `WorkDispatch` remains unchanged.
- Runner poll-only execution context flows `WorkDispatchResponse` -> `RenderedWorkItem` -> `ActionContext`; it does not belong in `with`, variables, prompts, or persisted workflow/session state.
