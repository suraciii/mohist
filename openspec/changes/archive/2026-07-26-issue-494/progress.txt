## Codebase Patterns

- Durable event handlers are discovered from `ICloudEventHandler` plus `SubscriptionAttribute` and exposed as `IEnumerable<Subscription>`.
- Web EventBridge now uses the separate `ICloudEventPushHandler` plus `EventPushAttribute`; it remains responsible for subscriber filtering and the existing CloudEvent envelope.
- Push delivery is process-local: `EventPushQueue.TryEnqueue` is synchronous and bounded, while `EventPushWorker` owns handler matching, TimeProvider-backed cancellation, and failure containment.
